Frequently Asked Questions

What causes subfloor squeaks and how do screws prevent them?
Squeaks occur when smooth-shank nails loosen slightly over time as joists shrink and expand with seasonal humidity. When walked on, the plywood rubs up and down along the loose nail shank. High-tensile coarse-threaded subfloor screws combined with polyurethane subfloor adhesive create a permanent monolithic bond that never loosens.
Why is an 1/8-inch expansion gap required between subfloor sheets?
Engineered wood panels (OSB and plywood) expand slightly when absorbing atmospheric humidity. If sheets are butted tightly together with no gap, the edges buckle upward at the seams, creating noticeable ridges under carpet, tile, or hardwood flooring.
What is the APA standard screw spacing for subfloors?
APA (The Engineered Wood Association) specifies screws spaced 6 inches apart along all panel supported edges, and 12 inches apart across intermediate interior joists (the field).
